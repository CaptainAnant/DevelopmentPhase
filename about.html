About ClasSync

ClasSync is a unified platform designed to streamline the academic lives of students. Born from a passion for technology and a desire to solve real-world problems, our vision is to create a single, reliable hub for everything a student needs—from real-time attendance tracking and resource management to notices and task organization.

Our Team: Trifecta

We are **Trifecta**, a passionate team of third-year undergraduate students and aspiring full-stack developers. We are actively building projects, mastering new technologies, and strengthening our skills in data structures, web development, and more. We believe in crafting innovative solutions and are eager to contribute to the ever-evolving tech world.
